Large Language Models, often abbreviated as LLMs, are advanced neural networks trained on massive amounts of text data.

They use transformer architectures to understand and generate human-like language.

These models can perform tasks such as text generation, summarization, translation, and question answering.

Despite their capabilities, LLMs can sometimes produce incorrect or fabricated information.

This phenomenon is known as hallucination.

Techniques like Retrieval Augmented Generation help reduce this issue by grounding responses in real data.
